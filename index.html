Workflow

How BloodConnect Operates

A transparent and secure workflow connecting volunteers with urgent medical requirements.

1

Register Profile

Sign up in seconds, record your blood group, and easily toggle your donation availability status on or off.

2

Submit Request

Patients or hospitals submit blood requirements with patient name, required units, hospital, and priority.

3

Admin Verification

Admin officers verify the authenticity of every request before it is published to the public network.

4

Connect & Save Lives

Donors click "I Can Help", exchange details safely, complete the donation, and log donation history.
